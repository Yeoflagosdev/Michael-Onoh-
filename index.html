The person behind Five One

Michael Onoh

Founder · Five One

Five One Agency was born in 2025 from a simple vision: to make campaigns feel more alive, dynamic, and authentically connected to the brands they represent. Rooted in originality and a passion for creative storytelling, Five One was built to push boundaries, not repeat familiar formulas. We believe every brand has a story worth telling and every campaign deserves an identity of its own. Through thoughtful direction and cinematic craft, we turn ideas into visual experiences that connect, resonate, and leave a lasting impression. We don’t just create campaigns. We create visual experiences that give ideas a life of their own.
